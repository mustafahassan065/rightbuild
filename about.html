Who We Are

A Legacy Built On Quality, Trust & Precision

Founded in 2010, BuildRight Construction started as a small family business with one belief: every project — no matter the size — deserves the highest standard of craftsmanship. Today, we're a team of 120+ engineers, architects and skilled tradespeople who have completed over 350 projects across California.

From custom dream homes to large-scale commercial developments, we bring the same dedication to every build: transparent communication, rigorous safety standards and an obsession with detail.
